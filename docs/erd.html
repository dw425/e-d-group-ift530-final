Data volumes

The assignment sets the load: 10 rows in every dimension table and 20–50 in every transactional table. The data is laid out by hand to stay analytically useful at that size — the window crosses a month boundary, sessions cluster onto nine days, and the payments that trip the fraud view are planted deliberately.
